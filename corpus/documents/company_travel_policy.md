# Global Corp Travel & Expense Policy (2025)

## 1. Flight Bookings
- Domestic travel: All domestic flights must be booked in Economy Class at least 14 days in advance.
- International travel: Business Class is permitted for continuous flights exceeding 8 hours with VP approval.
- Preferred booking tool: Employees must use the Corporate Travel Portal (Navan) for all itinerary reservations.

## 2. Lodging & Per Diem
- Hotel room rate caps:
  - Tier 1 Cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo): Up to $300/night before taxes.
  - Tier 2 Cities (Austin, Chicago, Berlin, Singapore): Up to $220/night.
  - All other locations: Up to $175/night.
- Daily Meal Per Diem:
  - Standard per diem is $75 per day ($15 Breakfast, $25 Lunch, $35 Dinner).
  - Alcohol is non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment dinner.

## 3. Expense Submissions
- Receipts are mandatory for all individual transactions exceeding $25.
- Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days of travel completion.
- Contact for queries: finance-travel@globalcorp.internal
